## 1. What supply chain risks does Apple describe in its risk factors?

Apple describes the following supply chain risks in its risk factors:

1. Disruptions in the supply of critical components due to severe financial problems or other disruptions in the business of outsourcing partners or suppliers [AAPL-FY2024#0034].
2. Industry-wide shortages and significant commodity pricing fluctuations that can materially adversely affect the Company's business, results of operations, and financial condition [AAPL-FY2024#0023].
3. Restrictions on international trade, such as tariffs and other controls on imports or exports of goods, technology, or data, that can materially adversely affect the Company's business and supply chain [AAPL-FY2024#0023].
4. Global and regional economic conditions that can affect the Company's business, results of operations, financial condition, and stock price [AAPL-FY2025#0021].
5. Disruptions in the global supply chain due to geopolitical tensions or conflict, which can result in significant disruptions [AAPL-FY2024#0025].

## 6. What credit risk concerns does JPMorgan Chase raise in its risk factors?

JPMorgan Chase raises concerns about credit risk due to:

* Concentration or contagion risks, including the potential for widespread defaults on consumer debt, which could lead to recessionary conditions in the U.S. economy [JPM-FY2024#0080].
* Interrelationships between counterparties, borrowers, or obligors in similar, related, or dependent industries, which could exacerbate credit, liquidity, and market risk exposure [JPM-FY2024#0079].
* Excessive expansion of consumer credit by bank or non-bank competitors, which could prompt industry-wide reactions and impair JPMorganChase's ability to repay obligations [JPM-FY2024#0080].
* Adverse economic and market events and conditions, which could negatively affect JPMorganChase's ability to deliver products and services to its clients and customers, and weaken its results of operations and financial condition or credit ratings [JPM-FY2025#0060].

## 10. How does Apple describe the performance of its Services segment?

The Company allocates revenue and any related discounts to all of its performance obligations based on their relative SSPs. Because the Company lacks observable prices for product-related bundled services, the allocation of revenue is based on the Company’s estimated SSPs. Revenue allocated to the delivered hardware and bundled software is recognized when control has transferred to the customer, which generally occurs when the product is shipped. Revenue allocated to product-related bundled services is deferred and recognized on a straight-line basis over the estimated period [AAPL-FY2024#0113].
